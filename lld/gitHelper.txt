ssh-add -l

ls -al ~/.ssh

#Create new public key

ssh-keygen -t ed25519 -C "your_email@example.com"

📋 Step 3: Copy the public key

Display your public key:

cat ~/.ssh/id_ed25519.pub


(or cat ~/.ssh/id_rsa.pub if you used RSA)

Copy the entire output — it starts with ssh-ed25519 or ssh-rsa.

🌐 Step 4: Add the key to your Git hosting service
🔹 For GitHub:

Go to https://github.com/settings/keys

Click New SSH key

Give it a name (like “My Laptop”)

Paste your copied key

Click Add SSH key

🔹 For GitLab:

Go to https://gitlab.com/-/profile/keys

Paste your key and save.

🔹 For Bitbucket:

Go to https://bitbucket.org/account/settings/ssh-keys/

Click Add key, paste it, and save.
